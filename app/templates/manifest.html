Rules that bite

Send only what changed The submission is applied over the manifest in force. An unmentioned field keeps its value; a field set to null is cleared.
Unknown fields are refused {"verison": "2.0.0"} gets a 422. Silently accepting a typo would say the update landed when it had not — and, because a submission retires the one before it, a real update awaiting review would be thrown away by that typo.
One at a time Sending another replaces the one awaiting review. That is changing your mind, not queueing a second review.
Under 10,000 characters Counted on the JSON form of what is sent, and again on what it resolves to.
